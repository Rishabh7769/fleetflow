# FleetFlow

## Mission

Help small and medium transport companies replace Excel and paper records with a modern fleet management platform.

---

## Target Customers

- Transport companies with 5–50 vehicles
- Logistics businesses
- Fleet owners
- School bus operators (future)

---

## User Roles

### Owner
- View dashboard
- Manage users
- View reports
- Manage company

### Fleet Manager
- Add vehicles
- Assign drivers
- Create trips
- Manage maintenance

### Driver
- View assigned trips
- Update trip status
- Upload fuel receipts
- Report vehicle issues

---

## MVP Modules
